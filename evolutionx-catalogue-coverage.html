Catalogue coverage check

How much of your catalogue can enrich itself?

Your e-automate items carry operational data: a number, a description, a cost. A storefront needs images, brands, categories and copy. Where an item matches a SKU in a managed catalogue you subscribe to, all of that arrives automatically. This tool reads your exports and tells you how many items are covered, which catalogue covers them, and how many your team will have to enrich by hand.

1

Add your exports

In e-views, run two system views — Items.EvolutionX and InventoryVendorItemNumbers.EvolutionX — and export each one as a CSV. No filters needed: export all the results from both. Drop the two files below, along with your catalogue exports from step 2. Nothing leaves your computer; the analysis runs entirely in this page.

Drop your CSV files here

or browse for them  ·  add them all at once, or a few at a time

2

Import your catalogues

Sign in to EvolutionX admin, go to Catalogs, then Available Catalogs. Open your vendor managed catalogue and click Export. Download the file as a CSV and add it in step 1. Repeat for every catalogue you subscribe to.

Once your catalogue files are in, they will appear here to be set up.

3

Define your pricing matrixes (optional)

Only items that are active and e-info enabled are counted, whatever you choose here. If you only put part of your inventory online, narrow it down by pricing matrix — otherwise leave everything selected. (blank) covers items with no pricing matrix assigned.

Add your e-automate items export above to choose pricing matrices.
